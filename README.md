## 1. Research Overview

The increasing use of Internet of Things (IoT) devices has created a need to protect the data collected, processed and exchanged by connected devices. However, many IoT devices operate with limited processing capability, memory and energy resources. These limitations can affect the implementation and performance of encryption techniques.

Lightweight encryption is therefore an important research direction for resource-constrained IoT environments. This research proposes a comparative evaluation of selected lightweight encryption algorithms under consistent experimental conditions. The study will focus on comparing their performance and resource-efficiency characteristics.

---

## 2. Research Problem

### Problem 1: Lack of Standardised Comparison

Existing studies often evaluate encryption algorithms using different hardware platforms, datasets, workloads and evaluation metrics. These differences make it difficult to directly compare the performance and resource requirements of different lightweight encryption algorithms.

### Problem 2: Limited Evaluation Across Diverse Conditions

Many existing studies focus on specific hardware platforms, datasets or controlled experimental environments. This creates a need for further evaluation using consistent experimental conditions to better understand the performance and resource-efficiency characteristics of lightweight encryption algorithms in resource-constrained IoT environments.

---

## 3. Research Aim

This research aims to comparatively evaluate selected lightweight encryption algorithms for resource-constrained IoT devices based on their performance and resource-efficiency characteristics under consistent experimental conditions.

---

## 4. Research Objectives

The research has three objectives:

1. **To identify and select lightweight encryption algorithms suitable for resource-constrained IoT environments based on findings from existing literature.**

2. **To implement the selected lightweight encryption algorithms in a controlled experimental environment using consistent test conditions.**

3. **To evaluate and compare the selected algorithms based on defined performance and resource-efficiency metrics.**

---

## 5. Proposed Algorithms

The proposed study will focus on the following encryption algorithms:

- **AES-128**
- **SPECK**
- **ASCON**

The final implementation configuration will be determined based on the experimental environment and technical feasibility.
